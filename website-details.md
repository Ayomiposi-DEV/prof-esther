Short Bio:
Esther O. Ajayi-Lowo, PhD, is an Assistant Professor of Women and Gender Studies at Texas Christian University. Her passion as a scholar-activist-teacher lies at the intersection of gender, race, healthcare, and social justice. She is a full-spectrum doula with research interests in Indigenous Birthing Epistemologies, African Feminisms, Transnational Feminism, Black Maternal Health, and Global Reproductive Justice. Dr. Ajayi-Lowo’s research has been supported by prestigious institutions, including the Social Science Research Council (SSRC) and the American Association of University Women (AAUW). She has published on sexuality, reproductive justice, African and African Diasporic Birthing, and decolonial pedagogy more broadly. Her recent article “Safe Motherhood Initiative: Whither African Indigenous Birthing Knowledge?” was published in Meridians in 2024. Dr. Ajayi-Lowo is currently rounding off her book manuscript, Decolonizing Childbirth: Mothers, Midwives, and Modernity in Nigeria, an ethnographic exploration of contemporary African Indigenous birthing epistemologies and approaches.

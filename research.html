Core Research Areas

Dr. Ajayi-Lowo’s scholarly agenda interrogates how indigenous philosophies and feminist frameworks dismantle entrenched inequities in healthcare, education, and human rights.

African Indigenous Birthing Knowledge

Interrogating the historical marginalization of African Indigenous birthing knowledges within global public health discourses like the Safe Motherhood Initiative. Investigating the cultural, spiritual, and embodied wisdom of traditional birth attendants (TBAs) and how indigenous midwifery offers restorative models for contemporary birth.

Meridians 2024 Bloomsbury 2022

African & Transnational Feminisms

Tracing decolonial feminist genealogies across Africa and the African Diaspora. Examining how women resist colonial legal frameworks, patriarchal body policing (e.g., Nigeria’s Same-Sex Marriage Prohibition Act), and systemic gender-based discrimination through grassroots organizing and transnational solidarity.

Agenda 2025 Brill Sense 2018

Black Maternal Health & Birthing Complexities

Analyzing racial disparities, institutional neglect, and the over-medicalization of maternal healthcare. Synthesizing lessons from COVID-19 to champion holistic doula support, maternal health advocacy, and community-centered birthing rights for Black women across the United States and the Global South.

YWCA Health Equity TWU Symposium

Decolonial Pedagogy & Reproductive Justice

Bridging academic theory with social activism in university classrooms. Developing decolonial curricula for Women and Gender Studies, advancing comprehensive sexuality education as a reproductive health right, and empowering students to negotiate power, privilege, and social transformation.

Univ of Illinois Press 2025 Teaching Justice 2016
